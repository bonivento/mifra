## Brand & Style

The design system projects total purity, scientific efficacy, and pristine sanitation. Tailored for commercial maintenance managers, hospitality pool operators, and regional institutional clients in Valledupar, the visual language balances clinical precision with an invigorating, tropical-fresh aesthetic.

The style fuses **Modern Corporate Clarity** with **Hydro-tactile Freshness**:
- Crisp, clinical structure with generous breathing room reflecting immaculate spaces.
- Dynamic aquatic micro-interactions and luminous translucent surfaces evocative of treated pool waters and concentrated cleaning solutions.
- High-contrast operational triggers designed for rapid dispatch, instant chemical inquiry, and frictionless WhatsApp commerce.
- Absolute clarity and legibility under direct sunlight on handheld devices in industrial, humid, and outdoor facility environments.

## Colors

The palette derives from pure treated water, sterile surfaces, and active disinfection chemistry.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0284C7` - Aquatic Pool Blue)**: Conveys filtration, chlorinated cleanliness, and liquid flow. Used for core brand elements, primary CTAs, links, and active interactive strokes.
- **Secondary (`#0369A1` - Deep Marine)**: Grounding tone for visual hierarchy, data-dense metrics, technical headers, and strong button active states.
- **Tertiary (`#84CC16` - Active Lime)**: Represents biocidal strength, natural citrus scents, and fresh sanitation. Reserved for freshness badges, chemical safety indicators, stock availability, and high-conversion WhatsApp direct triggers.
- **Cyan Accent (`#06B6D4`)**: Expresses aerated water and purity; applied to glow states, ambient gradients, and highlight ribbons.
- **Neutral Stack (`#0F172A` to `#F8FAFC`)**:
  - `#0F172A` (Slate 900): Deep industrial text color for maximum daylight legibility.
  - `#334155` (Slate 700): Secondary technical specifications, CAS numbers, usage dilution text.
  - `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200): Precise structural container dividers.
  - `#F8FAFC` (Slate 50): Bleached sanitary surface background.
  - `#FFFFFF` (Crisp White): Foreground cards, floating toolbars, and input surfaces.

## Typography

The pairing of **Plus Jakarta Sans** and **Inter** bridges approachable Caribbean commercial hospitality with rigorous industrial engineering.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** brings sculptured clarity, rounded geometry, and modern vigor to headings, product titles, and high-impact value propositions.
- **Inter** provides uncompromised legibility for chemical concentration percentages, safety warnings, volume pricing tables, and inventory statuses.
- Use `label-technical` with uppercase styling and tabular lining figures for chemical purity ratios (e.g., *90% Cl*, *pH 7.2 - 7.6*), SKU codes, and batch certifications.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured fluid layout system ensures clarity across busy procurement desks and on-site smartphones in bright daylight.

### Layout Rhythm
- **Desktop Grid (1024px+)**: 12-column layout, max-width `1280px`, with `gutter: 1.5rem` and `margin: 2rem`. Product catalogs leverage a 3- or 4-column rhythmic matrix.
- **Tablet Grid (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 1.5rem`.
- **Mobile Grid (<768px)**: 4-column layout, `gutter-sm: 1rem`, `margin-sm: 1rem`. Element groupings stack into fluid single-column sequences with strict minimum touch zones (48px).

Vertical rhythm follows modular multiples of `space-md` (16px). Maintain expanded empty breathing margins (`space-xl`) between technical data tables and visual marketing displays to preserve a calm, sterile aesthetic.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth mimics light passing through crystal-clear chlorinated water into white architectural basins. Traditional heavy drop shadows are replaced with soft aquatic luminescence and pristine surface separation.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Foundation)**: Solid `#F8FAFC`. Base canvas simulating sterile lab surfaces.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & Tables)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` with hairline perimeter outline (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) and ambient micro-diffusion: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.04), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating & Hover States)**: `box-shadow: 0 12px 24px -6px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.08)`. Subtle aquatic cyan-blue glow that elevates items during hover.
- **Tier 3 (Persistent Floating Actions & Overlays)**: Fast-order triggers, chemical dosage calculators, and fixed WhatsApp anchors use: `0 20px 30px -10px rgba(3, 105, 161, 0.20), 0 8px 10px -4px rgba(132, 204, 22, 0.15)`.
- **Glass Tinting**: Secondary sub-navigation bars and quick filters use `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a bottom border `1px solid rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The geometry balances hydro-dynamic ergonomics with industrial stability.

- **Level 2 (Rounded)**:
  - Base interactive elements (inputs, standard buttons, chemical spec chips): `0.5rem` (8px).
  - Cards, modal dialogs, and product sheets (`rounded-lg`): `1rem` (16px).
  - Hero container callouts and interactive product banners (`rounded-xl`): `1.5rem` (24px).
  - Badges, status pills, and direct WhatsApp dispatch buttons retain a full pill contour (`9999px`) to express tactile fluidity and rapid action.
